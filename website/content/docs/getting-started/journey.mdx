---
title: From URL to the stores
description: "The core path: create, preview, publish, update."
---

The path has four steps, each with its own guide. Every step works from
the dashboard, the CLI, or the MCP server.

## 1. Create the app

The input is the site's URL, a name, and an icon; nothing else is
required. The app loads the site as it is.
[Create your app →](/guides/create-app)

## 2. Preview it on a phone

The preview is one link that works on every phone; from a computer it is
scanned as a QR code. When it is available depends on who adapts the
website to the app. Apps created from the dashboard are prepared by
Appo's team by default, typically within 24 to 48 hours. From the CLI or
an AI assistant, the app is self-managed when you or your developer
maintain the website's code: it skips the Appo preparation step and can
be tried within minutes. Otherwise Appo's team prepares it, typically
within 24 to 48 hours.
[See the preview →](/guides/preview)

## 3. Publish

Appo runs the operated publication to the selected stores: builds,
submission, Apple review. Each gate requires the owner's approval.
[Publish to the stores →](/guides/publish)

## 4. Keep it updated

Web deploys reach the app without any store action, because the app
loads the site. Changes to the name, icon, or splash screen require a
new operated build.
[Update the app →](/guides/update)

If a store review fails, Appo converts the rejection into a required
action and a fix recipe: [recover from a rejection](/guides/rejection).
