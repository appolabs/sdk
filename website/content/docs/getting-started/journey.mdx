description: "The core path: create, preview in 48 hours, publish, keep it updated."
---

Four steps, each with its own guide. Every step works from the dashboard,
the CLI or the MCP server.

## 1. Create the app

Give Appo your site's URL, a name and an icon. That is the whole input:
your site is the app.
[Create your app →](/guides/create-app)

## 2. Preview it on your phone

Within 48 hours you get an installable preview: TestFlight on iOS, a deep
link on Android, a QR to reach both.
[See the preview →](/guides/preview)

## 3. Publish

Appo starts the operated publication to the stores you choose: builds,
submission, Apple review. You approve at the gates.
[Publish to the stores →](/guides/publish)

## 4. Keep it updated

Deploy your site and the app follows: every deploy is already in the app.
Name, icon or splash changes get a new operated build.
[Update the app →](/guides/update)

Something went wrong in review? Appo curates the rejection into a required
action and a fix recipe: [recover from a rejection](/guides/rejection).
