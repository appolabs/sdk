---
title: Preview on device
description: Try your app on your phone before publishing, at no extra cost, through one preview link from the dashboard, the CLI or an AI assistant.
---

The preview installs your app on a real phone before it is published, at no
extra cost. It is one link that works on every phone: opened on the phone, it
installs the right version for it; opened on a computer, it shows a QR code to
scan with the phone.

## When the preview is available

It depends on who adapts the website to the app:

- **You or your developer**, when you maintain the website's code
  (self-managed): the app can be tried right away. The phone version is built
  the first time the app is tried, which takes a few minutes.
- **Appo's team**: the app can be tried once the team has adapted the website,
  typically within 24 to 48 hours.

Apps created from the dashboard are prepared by Appo's team by default. From
the CLI or an AI assistant, you choose when the app is created.

## From the dashboard

1. Open your app on [apps.goappo.io](https://apps.goappo.io).
2. Select **Try on your phone**. On a phone, the preview page opens; on a
   computer, the same link is shown as a QR code.

## From an AI assistant or the CLI

Ask your assistant to let you try the app. It calls `preview_app`, which
returns the state of the preview (`ready`, `preparing`, `building` or `failed`)
with a message for you. When the state is `ready`, the answer also contains:

- `preview_url`: the preview link, valid on every phone;
- `preview_qr`: the same link as a QR code drawn in text, for assistants that
  run in a terminal;
- `how_to_open`: a short note on how to open the link.

While the app is not ready, the answer lists what you can do, for example wait
for Appo's team or make the fixes yourself.

From the command line:

```bash
appo run [id]
```

Prints the preview link and its QR code, or what Appo is still doing; it starts
no build. If `id` is omitted, the command uses your only app, or shows a picker
when the account has several.

## On the phone

### Android

The preview page prepares the Android app, which takes a few minutes; keep the
page open. The app is then downloaded: open the downloaded file to finish the
installation. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from the browser.

### iPhone, when you maintain the website's code

The app installs directly on the iPhone, without TestFlight. Apple allows this
kind of installation only on devices registered for testing, so the first time
the iPhone is registered:

1. Open the preview link on the iPhone and tap **Install app**. The page asks
   you to register the iPhone first: tap **Register this iPhone**.
2. On the registration page, tap **Install profile** and allow the download.
3. Open **Settings**, tap **Profile Downloaded** at the top, then **Install**.
   If that entry is gone, the profile is under **Settings > General > VPN &
   Device Management**. The profile sends Appo the iPhone's device identifier
   (UDID), model and iOS version; it adds no app or icon to the Home Screen.
4. Go back to the preview page (**Back to your app**). Appo prepares the app for
   the registered iPhone, which takes a few minutes; keep the page open.
5. The installation starts: confirm in the iOS dialog. The app's icon appears
   on the Home Screen within a few seconds.

Registration is needed once per iPhone and Appo account. If the page asks
again on an iPhone that is already registered, choose **Already registered?
Install**. The number of devices that can be registered for testing is
limited by Apple. When the limit has been reached and none of your iPhones is
registered, the page says that the iOS preview is not available; the Android
preview is not affected.

### iPhone, when Appo's team prepares the app

The preview page opens the app in Apple's TestFlight app; if TestFlight is not
installed, install it from the App Store. Until the TestFlight build is available,
the page says that Appo is setting up iPhone testing and opens the app as soon
as it is ready.
