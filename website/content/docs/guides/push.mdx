---
title: Send a push notification
description: Deliver a push notification to your app's devices.
---

A push notification reaches every device that has your app installed and has
granted permission. You can target a URL, attach an image, and schedule
delivery.

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI', 'MCP']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

1. Open your app on [apps.goappo.io](https://apps.goappo.io).
2. Go to the push notifications section. {/* verify: dashboard */}
3. Enter a title, body, and optional target URL, image, or schedule.
4. Confirm. Appo dispatches the notification immediately or at the scheduled
   time.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

```bash
appo push <id> --title <t> --body <b> [--target-url <u>] [--image-path <p>] [--scheduled-at <when>] --confirm
```

`--confirm` is required; without it the command prints a preview and exits.

</Tab>
<Tab value="MCP">

Ask your assistant to send a push notification: it calls `send_push`
(confirm-gated) with `title`, `body`, and any optional fields.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Push notifications are included on all plans; the Business plan removes
per-notification limits.
