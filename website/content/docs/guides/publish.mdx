---
title: Publish to the stores
description: Start the operated publication and follow it to approval.
---

Publishing is operated: Appo runs builds, store submission and Apple review;
each gate requires your approval. One prerequisite for the App Store: an Apple
Developer account in your name (Appo guides the enrollment).

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI', 'MCP']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

1. Open your app on [apps.goappo.io](https://apps.goappo.io).
2. Start the publication path and choose the target stores.
3. Confirm. Appo runs the remaining steps and shows the publication state on
   the app overview.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

`appo ship` registers the publish intent, with a confirm gate:

```bash
appo ship <id>
```

Follow progress with `appo status <id>`. A new app is created first with
`appo new` (see [Create an app](/guides/create-app)).

</Tab>
<Tab value="MCP">

Ask your assistant to publish: it calls `publish_app` (confirm-gated) and
reads progress with `get_app_overview`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Whatever the surface, the publication state and the next required action are
always visible: dashboard overview, `appo status`, or `get_app_overview`.
