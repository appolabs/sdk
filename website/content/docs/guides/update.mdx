---
title: Update an app
description: Keep your app's content and metadata in sync with your site.
---

Your app follows your site: web content updates are live as soon as the site
deploys, with no action required. Changes to the name, icon, or splash screen
trigger a new operated build by Appo.

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI', 'MCP']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

Content updates require no action: deploy your site and the app reflects the
change immediately.

For name or icon changes, open the app settings on
[apps.goappo.io](https://apps.goappo.io) and update the fields. Appo starts a
new operated build automatically.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

```bash
appo apps update <id> [--name <n>] [--url <u>] [--icon <https-url>]
```

Omit any flag to leave that field unchanged. Passing `--name` or `--icon`
triggers a new operated build; `--url` alone is a content-only change.

</Tab>
<Tab value="MCP">

Ask your assistant to update content fields: it calls `configure_app` with
`name` and/or `base_url`. For icon changes, it calls `set_icon`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Native changes — name, icon, and splash screen — require a new build operated
by Appo. Web content changes are live instantly with no build step.
