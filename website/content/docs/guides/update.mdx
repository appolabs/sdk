---
title: Update an app
description: Keep your app's content and metadata in sync with your site.
---

Your app follows your site: web content updates are live as soon as the site
deploys, with no action required. Name and icon changes update the record
immediately and reach devices with the next operated build, which Appo runs.

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI', 'MCP']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

Content updates require no action: deploy your site and the app reflects the
change immediately.

For name or icon changes, open the app settings on
[apps.goappo.io](https://apps.goappo.io) and update the fields. The record
updates immediately; devices receive the change with the next operated build.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

```bash
appo apps update <id> [--name <n>] [--url <u>] [--icon <https-url>]
```

Omit any flag to leave that field unchanged. `--url` alone is a content-only
change; `--name` and `--icon` update the record and reach devices with the
next operated build.

</Tab>
<Tab value="MCP">

Ask your assistant to update content fields: it calls `configure_app` with
`name` and/or `base_url`. For icon changes, it calls `set_icon`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Web changes are live as soon as the site deploys. Name and icon updates are applied to the record immediately and reach devices with the next operated build, which Appo runs.
