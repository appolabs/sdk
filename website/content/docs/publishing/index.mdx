Appo handles the entire build and publishing pipeline. Once your app is ready, Appo builds native binaries and publishes them to the App Store and Play Store on your behalf.

## Workflow

1. **Create the app** — Provide the website URL from the dashboard, the CLI (`appo new`), or the MCP server
2. **App preparation** — For dashboard-created apps, Appo generates the app name, icon, and store listing metadata from the website; status moves to **Ready** when complete. Apps created from the CLI or the MCP server are self-managed and are Ready immediately.
3. **Preview** — Test the app on your device using the dashboard preview
4. **Publish** — Select your target stores (iOS, Android, or both), choose a subscription plan, and confirm
5. **Review** — Apple and Google review the submission
6. **Live** — App available to users

## Store Developer Accounts

- **App Store:** an Apple Developer Program membership ($99/year) in your name or your company's. The fee is charged by Apple and **is not included in the Appo plan**. If you don't have the account yet, Appo guides you through the setup, then handles the build, code signing, and submission using your credentials.
- **Play Store:** no account is needed. The Android app is published under Appo's Google Play developer account.

## Store Listing

Appo auto-generates your store listing metadata (name, description, keywords) from your website content. You can upload screenshots from the dashboard.

Store listing editing is being developed — currently the auto-generated metadata is reviewed and finalized by Appo before submission.

## What Appo Handles

- **Store listing** — Auto-generated from your website, reviewed before submission
- **Cloud builds** — Native binaries compiled in the cloud
- **Code signing** — Certificates and provisioning profiles managed automatically
- **Store submission** — Binaries uploaded and listings created in App Store Connect (with your developer credentials) and in Google Play Console
- **Updates** — Rebuild and resubmit when you update your app

## Subscription

Publishing requires an active Appo plan, selected during the publish flow. See the [available plans](https://goappo.io/pricing) for details. Subscriptions are managed through the dashboard and billed via Stripe.
